05 / Property Preservation

Protect vacant and foreclosed properties across the United States

Authorized work is completed according to the property condition, servicer work order, bid limits, local requirements, and approved scope. Final requirements vary by lender and contract.

Property inspections

Initial, occupancy, vacancy, and damage inspections with dated, geo-referenced photo reports and property-condition updates.

Securing & lock changes

Boarding and securing of doors and windows, lock changes, lockbox installation, and replacement of damaged entry hardware when authorized.

Cleaning & debris removal

Interior and exterior trash-out services, debris removal, abandoned-item cleanup, and broom-clean preparation of vacant properties.

Yard & exterior care

Lawn mowing, weed removal, hedge trimming, minor lot cleanup, and winter or seasonal exterior maintenance based on work order specifications.

Repairs & winterization

Minor property repairs, damage mitigation, plumbing and system winterization, roof tarping, and other approved preservation work.

Reporting & compliance

Work-order tracking, before-and-after evidence, bid and invoice support, exception alerts, and documented completion for audit-ready records.
